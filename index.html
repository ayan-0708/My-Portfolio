AICTE DKTE IDEA Lab: "Waste to Best"

Collaborative student initiatives transforming festival waste and digital ideas into sustainable solutions.

Collaborating for Sustainable Innovation

During Diwali, the AICTE DKTE IDEA Lab at DKTE Society's Textile and Engineering Institute organized a unique student-led innovation drive centered on the theme "Waste to Best".

Ayan Shaikh participated as an active member of this student initiative alongside peers across the Electronics & Telecommunication (ETC), Mechanical, and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI-DS) departments. Under the mentorship and encouragement of college leadership—including Director Prof. Dr. L. S. Admuthe, Deputy Director Prof. Dr. U. J. Patil, Dr. Suyog Rayjadhav, Dr. V. B. Kumbhar, and Prof. Digvijay Mhamane—students worked together to develop five distinct eco-friendly solutions.

Bloom Bag Artificial leather and reusable bags engineered from floral temple waste (nirmalya).
Seed Lantern Biodegradable sky lanterns embedded with seeds that sprout when planted in soil.
Regenerative Candle Reusable, clean-burning candles molded from recycled leftover wax.
EcoDiwali App Interactive digital fireworks, animations, and rangoli eliminating smoke and noise.
Pixel LED Fireworks Pollution-free electronic light displays simulating festive fireworks.
Shared Student Teamwork: This initiative was a collective triumph of team collaboration across engineering branches. It was guided by institute mentors and brought forward through the shared dedication of all participating students.
